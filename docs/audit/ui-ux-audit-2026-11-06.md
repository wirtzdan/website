# danielwirtz.com UI/UX audit, 2026-11-06

A polish audit of the live site (`https://danielwirtz.com`), driven with Playwright (Chromium 156) under xvfb between 00:20 and 01:20 UTC on 6 November 2026. Nothing was redesigned and no product code was changed. Every finding below was seen on the live site and has a screenshot.

`/`, `/about`, `/blog`, `/blog/gmail-url-paste`, `/blog/diy-co2-sensor-home-assistant`, `/blog/utrecht-ultra-2024-race-report`, `/books`, `/bookmarks`, `/pizza`, `/bread`, `/tools`, `/fuse-box`, `/photos`, `/newsletter`, `/outseta`, `/privacy`, `/disclaimer`, and a nonsense URL.

- `/blog/advanced-outseta-tips`: code blocks and reading progress

**Not covered, and why:**

- **A successful subscribe.** It would send a real confirmation email.
- **The suggestion form.** It isn't mounted on any page.
- **Real iOS/Android hardware.** Touch was emulated, so safe-area insets and the iOS keyboard were not exercised.
- **Outseta video playback.** The Wistia embed shows marketing chrome rather than a player (see T9).
- **Bookmark and book image loading.** Owned by another session, as briefed, so broken or blank bookmark thumbnails visible in screenshots are deliberately not listed.
- **Excluded as by design:** Notion drafts, post titles and slugs, the icon set, SEO, the Books toggle and heart, the theme-toggle sound.
